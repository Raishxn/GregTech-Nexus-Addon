# :pick: Void Miner (Steam Gate Aged)

| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|

## Steam Fluid Tiers
| Fluid | Output | Speed | Energy Cost |
|-------|:------:|:-----:|:-----------:|
| Normal Steam | 1x | 1x | 1x |
| Dense Supercritical | **2x** | **2x** | 1.5x |
| SuperHeated | **3x** | **3x** | 2x |
| Insanely Supercritical | **5x** | **5x** | 4x |

!!! tip `Best Value`
    **SuperHeated Steam** offers the best cost-benefit: 3x output and 3x speed for only 2x energy cost.
