# :bulb: Optimization Tips

## General Tips
- Use **Wireless Steam** to eliminate pipes and reduce lag
- **Wall Sharing** works between GTNA and GT base multiblocks
- **Huge Steam Buses** have more slots than regular buses
- Combine **Thread + Accelerate + Overclock** for maximum speed
- Start with the **Large Steam Alloy Smelter** (easiest multiblock)

## Steam Efficiency
- The **Large Steam Furnace** uses 50% less steam than normal
- **Mega Solar Boiler** provides free infinite steam (requires sunlight)
- Use Steel Wireless Hatches for maximum capacity
