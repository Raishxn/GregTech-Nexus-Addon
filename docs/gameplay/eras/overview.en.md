# :world_map: Eras Overview
GTNA organizes its progression into thematic eras:

1. **Primitive Steam Era** (ULV/Steam) - Bronze Age
2. **Steam and Steel Era** (LV/MV) - Industrial Revolution
3. **Hydraulic Era** (HV) - Hydraulic Power
4. **Transition Era** (EV) - Electrical Dawn
5. **Supercritical Steam Era** (IV) - Beyond Limits
6. **Elemental Era** (LuV+) - Elemental Mastery

Each era introduces new materials, machines, and mechanics that build upon the previous one.
