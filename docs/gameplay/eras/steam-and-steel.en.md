# :gear: Steam and Steel Era
**Tiers**: LV (32 EU/t) to MV (128 EU/t)

**Key Materials**: Stronze, Breel, Compressed Steam

**Multiblocks**: Large Steam Furnace, Crusher, Alloy Smelter, Mega Solar Boiler, Steam Cobbler, Stone Superheater, Steam Manufacturer, Steam Woodcutter

**Challenge**: Master the Steam Network and produce Stronze alloy

## Progression
1. Build a Steam Boiler (vanilla GT)
2. Craft Bronze Plated Bricks and Bronze Pipe Casings
3. Build your first multiblock (Large Steam Alloy Smelter)
4. Produce Stronze alloy for advanced casings
5. Build the Mega Solar Boiler for infinite steam
