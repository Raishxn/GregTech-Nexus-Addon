# GregTech Nexus Addon

<div style="text-align: center; margin: 2rem 0;">
  <h2 style="color: #9B59B6; font-size: 1.8rem;">:gear: The Nexus of Steam & Steel</h2>
  <p style="font-size: 1.1rem; color: #aaa; max-width: 700px; margin: 0 auto;">
    An expansion for GregTech CEu Modern that transforms how you generate energy, process materials, and build your factories. From primitive steam to cosmic technology.
  </p>
</div>

## :star2: Key Features

<div class="feature-grid" markdown>

<div class="feature-card" markdown>
### :fire: Steam Revolution
8+ steam multiblocks that expand early-game mechanics. Alloy smelting, crushing, woodcutting, and even void mining — all powered by steam.
</div>

<div class="feature-card" markdown>
### :zap: Custom Hatches
Thread Hatch (simultaneous recipes), Accelerate Hatch (duration reduction), Overclock Hatch (speed multiplier), and Advanced Parallel Hatch (up to 262K parallels).
</div>

<div class="feature-card" markdown>
### :globe_with_meridians: Wireless Steam
Transmit steam without pipes. Build Wireless Steam Hatches and share resources between multiblocks at any distance.
</div>

<div class="feature-card" markdown>
### :test_tube: Exclusive Materials
Stronze, Breel, Echoite, Clay Compound — new alloys and elements that unlock unique recipes and structures.
</div>

</div>

## :rocket: Quick Start

1. **Install** GregTech CEu Modern 1.20.1
2. **Download** GregTech Nexus Addon from [GitHub](https://github.com/Raishxn/GregTech-Nexus-Addon)
3. **Place the .jar** in the mods/ folder
4. **Start with** the [Large Steam Alloy Smelter](gameplay/machines/steam/large-steam-alloy-smelter.md) — the easiest multiblock!
